## Teoria da decisão (5)

- ★ **J. Neyman & E. S. Pearson (1933).** *On the Problem of the Most Efficient Tests of Statistical Hypotheses*. Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A, 231, 289–337. doi:[10.1098/rsta.1933.0009](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.1933.0009) — _grátis_
